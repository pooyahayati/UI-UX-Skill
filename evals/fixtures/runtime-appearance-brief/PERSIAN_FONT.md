# Persian appointment workspace

We are defining a new browser-based appointment-management product for Persian
speakers, primarily used on phones, also on desktop. Persian is its only product
language and it uses right-to-left layout. It will have an existing owner/admin
area with trusted owner permissions and tenant-scoped asset storage. We have not
selected a font, palette or spacing foundation yet; I do not know design terms.

Please recommend the initial design foundations and draft our DESIGN.md. The
owner should be able to upload and choose a replacement font later in Design and
Appearance settings without editing application code. Explain the implementation
and verification work needed for that feature. We need notes/forms, a schedule
table, an appointment chart and confirmation overlays to look consistent.

Only planning is authorized now. No source, font assets, prior approved visuals
or running application are supplied. Generated artifacts belong in the output
directory; do not install fonts/software, contact customers or publish anything.
