# Practice Steps

Guided browser learning application for Persian-speaking novice users, reading
one short lesson step and choosing its next action on a phone. Content is sparse;
the owner wants a welcoming personality, while learners need recognizable labels
and a clear task order. Avoid distracting continuous animation. Some users enlarge
text or enable reduced motion. Progress/status meaning is already settled.

Owner selected Material for the planned foundation refinement. Approved plum
brand, Vazirmatn family/regular/bold, spacing and radius values are supplied under
`learning` in TOKENS.json. Font binary paths are not included; no rendering proof
is supplied. Persian is the primary and only language, RTL; technical lesson IDs
retain their own reading order. The conversation language is not additional scope.
No initial sample or taste approval exists. Dark review must follow primary review.

An existing owner appearance surface supports the prepared outline icon family
and approved font assets; its permission/storage/consumer implementation is not
part of this brief. Do not claim new controls from planning. No new library,
account role or product workflow is authorized. Give a recommendation and source
mapping only, not implementation or simultaneous theme/language variants.
