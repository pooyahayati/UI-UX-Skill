# Queue Notes

Browser web application for technicians reviewing assigned work and recording
notes. Repetitive time-sensitive tasks, bright outdoor phone use and occasional
weak connectivity. Some users also work from a keyboard at a desk. Existing
draft recovery, validation, permissions and status text must retain their meaning.

Owner already selected Material for the planned foundation refinement. Approved
brand, typography, spacing and radius values are supplied under `operations` in
TOKENS.json. Existing font family Inter with regular/bold requested; asset paths
and actual files are not supplied. Do not infer font rendering or licensing proof.
Primary and only product language English, LTR. Dark treatment is planned later;
the primary visual revision has not been produced or approved. Labels, task
hierarchy and workflow are settled. The prepared outline icon family is inherited.

No administrator backend exists. The requested deliverable is a foundation plan
and value-source mapping, not code, new screens, storage or a settings panel.
The owner wants a professional recommendation without a technical questionnaire.
