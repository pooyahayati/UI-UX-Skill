## [3.2.0] - 2026-10-06

- Completed R0–R8: product-aware discovery/recommendations, living `DESIGN.md`, incremental decisions and scoped handoffs.
- Added sequential approval: one foundation-aligned responsive primary-language/direction sample, then dark, then needed/authorized secondary language/layout.
- Added bounded appearance/font/icon guidance and executable synthetic fixtures with private drafts, trusted validation, publication, history, recovery and rollback; not a production admin engine or arbitrary font-upload implementation.
- Completed R9.1–R9.6: optional, product-personalized Material activation/foundations/research, six component families, provider fit, samples and compatible runtime adapters.
- Preserved product isolation, stack/permission/data boundaries, prior configuration values and the conditional `persian-writing` specialist. No mandatory library/framework or extra specialist.
- Published after exact-candidate CI/package/security checks. Scoped independent/manual/runtime evidence retains historical-model, shared-session, F01 and device/assistive-technology limits; no universal conformance or fictional customer approval.
